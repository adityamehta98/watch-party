Watch together on a streaming service

Pick a service. It opens right here in the Watch Party window; sign in with your own account and press play on anything. Everyone else is brought to the same title and signs in with their own account. Needs the free Watch Party Sync extension (you and each guest).

Custom site: any website with a video

Paste the link of a page with a video. It opens inside everyone's Watch Party window, and only you control it: press play and it plays for everyone, in sync. Everyone watching needs the Watch Party Sync extension.

Your connection

Each viewer downloads the movie from your PC, so your upload speed has to cover the movie's bitrate once per viewer. Run a speed test and enter your upload number to get warnings in the library.
